# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, specific, and open to good-faith
disagreement. Keep discussions focused on the project and make room for
different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private information or submit
malicious code, credentials, or intentionally harmful content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this code.
They may also restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and
productive.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private GitHub reporting
options. If no private option is available, open an issue without including
private or sensitive details and ask for a maintainer response.
